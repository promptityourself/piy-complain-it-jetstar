Missed agent demo

A fake airline chat ("Demo Air", not Jetstar). It shows what happens when a live agent joins and you do not notice.

Demo Air Help Live chat

How to use the demo

  1. Optional: click 🔔 Load notifier, then click 🔔 Test sound in its panel.
  2. Click ▶ Start scenario.
  3. Click Send in the chat. You are now in the queue for a live agent.
  4. Switch to another window, the same as when you wait for Jetstar.
  5. Without the notifier, nothing tells you that the agent joined. The agent asks once, then closes the chat.
  6. With the notifier, the sound repeats until you come back. Reply in the chat to keep the agent.

What happened

    The wait and timeout values are invented for the demo. Jetstar's real timeouts are not known.
